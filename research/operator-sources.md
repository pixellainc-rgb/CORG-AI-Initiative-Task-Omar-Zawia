# Operator evidence — Find Your Casino Intelligence

Access date: 2026-10-30. Primary-source desktop research. No gambling accounts opened, no deposits or withdrawals tested.

## Evidence use

The reference ranking came from the parent browser session. All ten names have a Spanish-domain licensing source; this only establishes jurisdiction support, not individual eligibility. Other countries must fail the eligibility gate. Current withdrawal data is strongest for six operators: Platincasino, bwin, Casino777, TonyBet, PlayUZU and LeoVegas. Betinia has processing terms but no accessible public withdrawal-method table.

Do not infer payout guarantees from headline marketing, withdrawal support from deposit methods, unlimited withdrawals from a missing maximum, or current game inventory from licence categories. Review links and logos were intentionally left null for the parent reference-page capture.

## Material caveats

- **Platincasino:** two timing/payment tables on one page differ; use the contractual processing statement with bank settlement caveat, and only the explicit withdrawals table for confirmed methods.
- **bwin:** its official guide is dated 2023; the live help page currently warns of payment-verification delays.
- **Casino777:** the help timing table begins after approval; contractual processing is a separate stage. Large requests can take longer.
- **TonyBet:** detailed payment attributes come from an official blog rather than a logged-in cashier; confidence is medium for timings/limits.
- **PlayUZU:** published majority/minutes claim is conditional. Same FAQ gives conflicting document-review targets, so do not promise 30-minute or 12-hour verification. Only Visa/Mastercard method-specific withdrawal data was visible.
- **LeoVegas:** up to five business days is general processing, not a tested method-specific guarantee. The limits table does not specify a daily/monthly period.
- **Betinia:** the payments page showed no payments found; homepage payment logos are not withdrawal evidence.
- **Gran Madrid:** bank settlement is up to 72 business hours after approval; processing time and limits remain missing.
- **44Aces and Luckia:** jurisdiction evidence is available; commercial withdrawal attributes remain missing.
